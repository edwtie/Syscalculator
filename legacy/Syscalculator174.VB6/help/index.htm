Wizardexpress, Conversion Tool.

 

Wizardexpress is suitable for older Excel, Word, LibreOffice or other classic Office programs. LibreOffice has been tested and works ok. Microsoft 365 / Office 365 is known not to work reliably with this old VB6 integration. Dit feature will convert  your amounts in guildens to euro. It's a conversion tool. It like to feature  'Replace&Find'.  

1.  Make a new  colom with cell-property which you can set currecny euro 

2.  Mark all amounts (only values) and copy this marked amount. 

 

3. Submit  button "OK" to convert all amounts  Wait for green light.

4. Paste this converted amounts on new colom.

5. Ready !

 

Feature: Symbol. 

When you checked symbool, your value will added by symbool:  fl 351,53 -> €  241,22

Without symbol-feature  your amount will convert to new value without symbol.

Converting from Guildens to Deutsche Mark (Tri-Converation)

It's very easy to convert from guildens to Deutsche Mark or another curreny

Copy marked amounts and convert to euro using WizardExpress

Change:  NLG -> DEM

Choose:  Euro  in WizardExpress

Convert this amounts again to Deutsche Mark using WizardExpress.

Symb1 , Symb2 

Symbol will appear on Syscalculator.  Symb1 and Symb2 are symbol before values.

Symb3, Symb4 

Symb3 and Symb4 are symbol after values

For Example:

Symb2 DM
