# Syscalculator History

Syscalculator 2.0 is part of a longer Tiedragon/Tiedos software line. The
oldest recovered public source so far is the archived Tiedos homepage:

```text
https://web.archive.org/web/19970222204653/http://www.xs4all.nl/~edwtie/
```

The Wayback capture is from 22 February 1997 and shows a homepage created on
11 January 1997 for Tiedos Software.

## 1997: Nodelistomzetter and Nodomzet roots

The page mentions `Nodelistomzetter` version 1.36. It was aimed at BBS sysops
who needed to convert Dutch and French telephone numbers in nodelists. This is
the recovered public predecessor of the later conversion tooling and the
Nodomzet/NOD line.

Another historical name for the same idea is `Hong-technologie`: technology
made to convert or transform important user data. In current Syscalculator
terms, that is the conceptual predecessor of the NOD system. NOD is the
structured, readable form of that conversion idea.

The same page also describes a planned `Nodelistzoeker for Windows 95`.
`Nodelistomzetter` is described there as an internal part of that broader
Nodelistzoeker idea.

## 1997: MoneyCalculator

The page also mentions `MoneyCalculator`, then under development. It converted
guilders to euros and supported other European currencies that would disappear
with the euro transition. The page describes it as a variant based on the
Nodelistomzetter source.

## 1998-2001: TCSoftware, Hong technology and Syscal

Later recovered pages are:

```text
https://web.archive.org/web/20010720144922/http://www.famtie.tcsoftware.com/
https://web.archive.org/web/20010122031000/http://www.tcsoftware.com/product/syscallite.htm
https://web.archive.org/web/20010708203826/http://www.tcsoftware.com/down/index8.htm
```

That capture redirects to the Tiedos homepage of Edward Tie. The archived
frames and news pages show the transition from homepage/service work toward
Syscal/Syscalculator:

- 3 June 1998: Tiedos/TCSoftware moved to `www.tcsoftware.com`.
- 10 November 1998: the homepage moved to `www.famtie.tcsoftware.com`.
- 13 November 1998: `HONG-Technologie voor Syscal` is described as technology
  for converting or transforming important data. This is the clearest recovered
  wording for the idea that later became the NOD system.
- 1 January 1999: `FreeSyscal Euro Edition` is mentioned as free software for
  converting guilders to euros.
- 22 March 1999: `Syscalculcator Lite 1.5 Beta` is mentioned with a planned
  demonstration and a renewed interface.
- 18 June 1999: a new upgrade for `Syscalculcator Euro Edition` is mentioned,
  with support for 11 currencies instead of 8.
- January 2001: the `Syscalculcator Lite 1.48` product page says it descends
  from its predecessor `Nodomzet`.
- The same product page explains that, through Hong technology, Syscalculcator
  can calculate, renumber, convert or translate. It also mentions starting
  small `hong` programs and building dictionary/translation programs with
  `hongprogrammatje`.
- July 2001: the download page says Hong technology uses the `.nod` extension
  for all files and supports applications such as calculating, translating,
  converting and measuring.
- July 2001: the download page lists `Free syscal Euro Editon 1.50 Beta 2`,
  including an executable file and source-code file.
- A recovered screenshot of `Syscalculator Euro Edition` shows version
  `1.50.94 RC 2`, the `Eurorekenmachine` window, the small `WizardExpress`
  window and an About dialog with copyright `1996-2001`. The About dialog also
  states that the program is free software under the GNU General Public License,
  version 2 or later.
- 2001: the site also presents `Thuisnetwerk Service`, showing that the same
  software line was surrounded by practical networking, web and service work.

An archived `.nod` file from December 2002 (`CF-m3.nod`) shows the readable NOD
format in practice with commands such as `Name`, `indoprint`, `format`, `Symb3`,
`Symb4`, `input1`, `input2`, `Result`, `Resfou`, `math` and `end`.

This makes the early design line clear:

```text
Nodelistomzetter / Nodomzet
  -> Hong-technologie: important data conversion/transformation
  -> searchable/conversion-oriented BBS tooling
  -> MoneyCalculator currency conversion
  -> FreeSyscal / Syscalculator Euro Edition
  -> Syscalculcator Lite 1.x with .nod Hong programs
  -> Syscalculator/NOD conversion and calculation system
```

## Current line

Syscalculator 1.74 is the VB6 maintenance line for classic NOD compatibility.
Syscalculator 2.0 is the C#/.NET daily and beta line with NOD 2.0, Graph2D,
Graph3D, geometry, help tooling and Language Package 1.0.

The recovered 1997 page is useful as historical evidence that the project did
not start as a generic calculator. It grew from practical conversion software:
telephone/nodelist conversion first, then currency conversion, then the broader
NOD conversion and calculation model.
