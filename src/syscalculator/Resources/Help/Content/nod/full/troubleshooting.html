Common mistakes

  • Duplicate symbols: do not use both left and right symbols for the same value, otherwise you can get text such as DM 25 DM.
  • Introduction without end: close every indoprint block with indoend.
  • Math without ans: use ans as the current value, for example math ans * 100.
  • No end: place end at the end of the file.
  • Comma/dot: decimals often work with comma or dot; always test with F6.
