Status: this is not a normal NOD 2.0 beta feature. It belongs to Syscalculator 2.1 research: a formula-film generator where numbers, powers, brackets and symbols move as separate actors.

The current solver is still useful for simple step explanations, but the chain rule, product rule, quotient rule and integrals need a new animation API. That is why this topic lives in the formula cards as a demo and research point.
